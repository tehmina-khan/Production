Roof Waterproofing

Home Renovation

Completed extensive home renovation works, including demolition of existing bathrooms, laundry room, kitchen finishes, and ceilings, followed by layout alterations to create a study room, a refurbished laundry room, and a larger bathroom. Fit-Out & Finishing Work

Global Village

Client / Location: Turkish Pavilion, Global Village, Dubai

Executed comprehensive fit-out and finishing works at the Turkish Pavilion, Global Village, Dubai. Scope included cement board cladding, gypsum board partitions, surface preparation and painting, custom kiosk construction, flooring installation, suspended ceilings with metal framing channels, and masonry works, delivering coordinated installation, accurate alignment, and consistent finishes throughout the pavilion.
